# Live Debate Fact-Checker

An agent that checks claims as they are spoken in a debate and steps in by voice, changing how the debate proceeds.

## Language

**Director**:
The policy that decides whether and when the agent speaks.
_Avoid_: Moderator (for the decision-making), controller

**Moderator**:
The agent's on-air persona: the voice and dry referee tone heard by the room.
_Avoid_: Narrator, commentator, host

**Interjection**:
A single thing the Director has decided the Moderator should say.
_Avoid_: Interruption, comment
